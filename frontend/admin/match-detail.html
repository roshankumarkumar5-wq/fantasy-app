Match Detail

Lock Selections

Selection closes automatically at 11:59 PM on the day before the match (no manual action needed for that). Use this button if you want to lock it early for any reason.

Upload Scoresheet PDF (generates a CSV for you)

Upload the official match scoresheet PDF. It's stored for your records, and best-effort parsed into a CSV of runs/wickets/catches/stumpings/run-outs per player (works well with CricHeroes-style scorecards). Player names are the key — download the CSV, fix anything that looks wrong in Excel, then upload it in step 3.

Upload Final Stats CSV

Upload the CSV — either the one generated in step 2 (edited/corrected), or one you build yourself. Columns: player_name, player_id, runs, balls_faced, fours, sixes, is_out, wickets, bowled_lbw_wickets, maidens, overs_bowled, runs_conceded, catches, stumpings, run_outs, run_out_assists (player_id is optional — matching is done by name; is_out should be true or false; overs_bowled uses cricket notation e.g. 3.4 = 3 overs and 4 balls; run_outs = direct hit by one fielder, run_out_assists = combined run-out with 2+ fielders involved). This is what unlocks the Finalize step below.

Or enter stats manually

Alternative to the CSV upload above — add a row per player and pick them by name.

Finalize Match & Compute Points

Calculates every user's team total and marks the match completed. Blocked until final stats have been saved or uploaded (step 3 or the manual entry above).

Leaderboard

Ideal Team Combination

The highest-scoring fantasy squad that could have been picked for this match, based on the players' actual final performances and this match's own rules (composition, credit budget, special picks). For the admin's reference only.

Danger Zone

Permanently deletes this match and all associated user teams, stats, and leaderboard data. Only available for completed matches.
